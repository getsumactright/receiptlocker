## Scope

Home page of the ReceiptLocker marketing site (Persuade). Privacy and Support pages inherit the world.
Audience: one-person US local businesses on iPhone; action: the App Store badge; proof: the owner's
60 s film, real app screenshots, facts true of the shipping app (no mileage, no price figure, no
testimonials).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one thermal receipt printing down a night ground; every line on it is something
the app really does. It refuses the app-landing default of headline left, phone right, feature cards
below.

OWN-WORLD: Night #0C0C0D ground. One cool thermal-paper strip (#F3F3F0, never cream) with the logo's
sawtooth tear at its ends and section breaks, dashed rules, printed ink #1F2023 in a self-hosted
receipt mono for line items and totals. Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold for display on the night,
DM Sans for prose. Lime #C9F542 appears as a highlighter stroke on the one line that matters per
screen, never as decoration. Screenshots and the film are slips clipped or stapled to the roll.

STORY: The visitor reads the receipt top to bottom: scan, read on the device, filed to its Schedule
C line, totals by quarter, accountant pack, ACCOUNT/SERVER/TRACKING: NONE, TOTAL: READY. They watch
the film and tap the badge.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: nav (lockup left; How it works, Privacy, Support; badge right). Headline
"Receipts, ready for tax time." at ~6rem on the night, left. The receipt strip (~440px) feeds from
the top edge, centre. The film clipped at right as a 9:16 slip with a silent preview loop and a
play control. The App Store badge sits under the headline, not under the film: the action stays in
the reading order beside the promise, and the film carries its own play control. Mobile: headline
and badge, film slip, then the receipt full width.

FORM: The Receipt Roll; position 1 on the ordered list (pick card); seed key ca6cbc6d.
Signature interaction: the receipt feeds as you scroll, lines printing in like a print-head pass,
and the TOTAL highlighter swipes across when it arrives. Reduced motion: printed, no feed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
